Email GPS

Email GPS is a personal tool that keeps one person's own Gmail inbox organised. It makes sure a small set of Gmail filters and labels exist (calendar reminders, receipts, mail to respond to, mail waiting on a reply) and recreates any that go missing.

It has exactly one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else, there is no sign-up, and it is not a commercial product.

It runs on the owner's own computer and talks to Google only through the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission.
