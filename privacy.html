Email GPS privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses it

Email GPS has one user: the person who built it, using it on their own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's sign-in, it reads and changes the owner's Gmail filters and labels, applies labels to the owner's mail, and updates the owner's own Google Apps Script project and spreadsheet that do this work.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. The owner's mail and settings stay in the owner's own Google account. Email GPS does not copy mail to any other service, does not sell or share any data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model. The only thing kept outside Google is the sign-in key, stored on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner.

Google API data

Email GPS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Email GPS's access at any time from Google Account › Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Email GPS's Google sign-in screen.
